## Company Overview
Sapho Bio is a San Carlos, California biotechnology company, founded in 2024, that is building a rapid microbiology release-testing platform for precision medicine. The company's stated purpose is to accelerate pharmaceutical release testing so that precision medicines and other time-sensitive products can move from production to patients faster, on the conviction that release testing must evolve to match the speed and precision of modern therapeutics. Its offices are located at 1068 Washington Street, Suite B, San Carlos, CA.

## The Platform & Technology
Sapho Bio has developed an RNA-based rapid sterility test that runs on standard qPCR workflows and detects viable bacteria and fungi without requiring specialised instrumentation. The method returns results in under two days, with the underlying assay running on the order of twenty hours, whereas the traditional compendial sterility test takes fourteen days. The company reports that its assay has been validated to USP <1223> (the standard governing validation of alternative microbiological methods) across hundreds of samples for accuracy, precision, and limit of detection, and characterises each result as statistically non-inferior to the traditional USP <71> sterility test while being substantially faster.

The rapid sterility service is delivered commercially through the company's laboratory arm, Analytic Services Express, and a self-contained sterility testing kit is in development. Alongside sterility, the company's release and stability offering spans endotoxin and potency testing for compounding pharmacies and research-use-only vendors.

## Key Differentiators
- Speed without a compliance trade-off: results in under two days rather than two weeks, positioned as non-inferior to the compendial method and backed by USP <1223> validation.
- No specialised equipment: the assay runs on standard qPCR workflows already familiar to many laboratories, lowering the barrier to adoption.
- A release-testing focus purpose-built for short-shelf-life and precision-medicine products, where a fourteen-day sterility hold is operationally or clinically untenable.

## Target Audience
- 503B outsourcing facilities and sterile compounding pharmacies
- Research-use-only (RUO) vendors
- Precision-medicine, cell-therapy, and gene-therapy manufacturers whose products cannot wait out a two-week sterility hold *(assumption, inferred from the "precision medicine" positioning)*
- Pharmaceutical quality-control laboratories seeking faster lot release

## Value Proposition
Sapho Bio helps its customers:
- Release lots dramatically faster by compressing sterility turnaround from roughly fourteen days to under two, shortening beyond-use-date pressure and inventory holds.
- Adopt a rapid method with regulatory confidence, supported by USP <1223> validation and non-inferiority to USP <71>.
- Consolidate release and stability testing, sterility, endotoxin, and potency, with a single specialised partner.

## Recent Milestones
- **April 2026:** Secured $5M in funding and announced USP <1223> validation of its rapid sterility method. Reported investors include Mochi Health as a strategic partner alongside angel investors.
- Commercialisation of the rapid sterility service through Analytic Services Express; sterility testing kit in development.

## Key Messaging Points
1. **Speed that serves patients.** Faster release testing is not a convenience feature; for precision medicines it is the difference between a therapy reaching a patient in time or not.
2. **Rigour behind the speed.** Every result is positioned as statistically non-inferior to the traditional method, with USP <1223> validation as the evidence base.
3. **Built for how labs already work.** Standard qPCR workflows and no specialised equipment mean adoption without re-engineering a laboratory.
